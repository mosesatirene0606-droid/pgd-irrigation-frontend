What is Kc in agriculture?

Kc means Crop Coefficient.

In irrigation science (FAO-56), Kc is used to convert reference evapotranspiration
 (ET₀) into actual crop water use (ETc):

ETc = Kc × ET₀ 

If your supervisor asks “How was Kc obtained?”

You can confidently write:

“Crop coefficient (Kc) values used in this system
were adopted from FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56. 
The coefficients were selected based on crop type and growth stage, 
ensuring scientific accuracy in the estimation of crop evapotranspiration (ETc).”


How you will explain this in your thesis (EXACT WORDS)

You can copy this:
